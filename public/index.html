From clinical care to population health.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Dr. Uzzal led a team of nurses, medical assistants, and community health workers at Alikadam Upazila Health Complex — a project run by Gram Unnayon Sangathon (GRAUS) in partnership with Save the Children. It became the experience that turned his attention from the individual patient to the health of a whole population.

About

A physician who thinks in populations.

Dr. Uzzal Chandra Tangchangya is a general physician with over five years of experience working with local and international organisations, including BRAC, Save the Children, and the ICRC. He currently leads clinical care at the Medical Center of BRAC University's Residential Campus in Dhaka.

His path has taken him through emergency wards, digital health systems — including telemedicine, electronic health records, and digital prescription platforms — and a humanitarian COVID-19 response in the Chittagong Hill Tracts, where he extended clinical consultations to underserved communities. This experience raised questions clinical medicine alone couldn't answer, and ultimately led him into formal training in public health and epidemiology. He is currently pursuing an MPH in Epidemiology at the Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP) in Dhaka.
