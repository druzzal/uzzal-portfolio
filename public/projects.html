Case studies

Two programmes in the Chittagong Hill Tracts

A rural pandemic response run by GRAUS in partnership with Save the Children, and maternal and child health work with the ICRC in Naikhongchhari — both in Bandarban, both in 2020.

First case study

COVID-19 response — Alikadam Upazila Health Complex.

Role: Medical Officer Gram Unnayon Sangathon (GRAUS), with Save the Children Bandarban, Bangladesh Dec 2020 — Jun 2021

01 · Context

Between December 2020 and June 2021, Dr. Uzzal served as Medical Officer at Alikadam Upazila Health Complex in Bandarban — a COVID-19 project implemented by Gram Unnayon Sangathon (GRAUS) in collaboration with Save the Children.

02 · Challenge

A rural health complex needed to scale up its COVID-19 response with limited supplies and untrained staff.

03 · Intervention

The project supported the complex with medicines and instruments, and facilitated infection prevention and control and COVID-19 management training for the doctors and staff already working there.

04 · Leadership

As Medical Officer he led a team of nurses, medical assistants, and community health workers, managing daily clinic operations and keeping the complex running through the pandemic.

05 · Community

Health education programmes were arranged for community members alongside the clinical work — reaching people before they became patients.
